Try your own criteria

  • Change the area, period, sort metric and the two thresholds (tourist share, evening spending), and the table updates instantly.
  • Your settings stay in the address, so you can send the exact view to a colleague with 'Copy link'.

Evening, tourist-share, lodging and restaurant figures are only available as one-year totals, so they do not change with the period. The period applies to the tourist-visits column only. Click a neighborhood for all its figures.

Oreum crowd-spreading notices

  • Last autumn (Oct–Nov 2025), 56.8% of the 28,186 cars arriving at 95 oreums went to just three: Saebyeol Oreum, Geum Oreum, Yongnuni Oreum.
  • Pick a month and the notice changes. Copy it straight into social media posts, signs or text alerts.

Crowding (×) is a 5-year (2021–2025) average monthly index: ×1.0 is a normal month for that oreum, ×2.0 is twice as busy. Only oreums with 5,000+ arrivals over 5 years are included. 'Busier' oreums have an index of 1.3+ and are ordered by expected cars that month (yearly arrivals × index); 'alternatives' have 3,000+ arrivals a year, an index of 1.2 or below, and are not among that month's 3 busiest.

Busy months by neighborhood and where to redirect

  • Pick a neighborhood and month to see how far tourist visits that month exceed the neighborhood's monthly average.
  • Up to 3 of the 8 nearest neighborhoods are suggested: those whose rise that month is at most half as large and that have lodging spending.

'Nearby' is the distance between neighborhood centers on the map. Lodging share is the lodging portion of tourist card spending, a sign that an area has places to stay, not a room count. Areas under 1% lodging share and the islands (Udo, Chuja) are excluded.

Good to know

  • Card spending covers BC Card payments only, so it differs from total sales.
  • Tourist visits are based on phone signals, so one person can be counted more than once.
  • Oreum figures count only cars using navigation.
  • No year-on-year comparison, because the platform announced changes to its counting rules (2026-05-15, 2026-09-07).
  • Yongdam 2-dong contains Jeju International Airport, so its visits and spending largely reflect airport use.
