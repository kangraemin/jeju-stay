FAQ

Is Jeongbang-dong busy with tourists?

Domestic tourists visited 1.8M times in one year (Aug 2025–Jul 2026), #26 of Jeju's 43 neighborhoods.

Is Jeongbang-dong lively in the evening?

60.2% of tourist card spending happens between 6 pm and midnight, #1 of 43.

Is Jeongbang-dong a tourist area or a local area?

Tourists account for 51.3% of card sales there, so it is a mix of tourists and locals.
