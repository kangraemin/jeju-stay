FAQ

Is Bonggae-dong busy with tourists?

Domestic tourists visited 1.4M times in one year (Aug 2025–Jul 2026), #32 of Jeju's 43 neighborhoods.

Is Bonggae-dong lively in the evening?

24.6% of tourist card spending happens between 6 pm and midnight, #34 of 43.

Is Bonggae-dong a tourist area or a local area?

Tourists account for 34.4% of card sales there, so it is mostly a local residential area.

When is Bonggae-dong least crowded?

On a 5-year average (2019, 2022–2025), the quietest month is Mar and the busiest is Oct.

Has Bonggae-dong grown since before COVID?

Tourist visits in 2025 were 18% lower than in 2019 (#39 of 43).

Who should stay in Bonggae-dong?
