FAQ

Is Ara-dong busy with tourists?

Domestic tourists visited 2.7M times in one year (Aug 2025–Jul 2026), #21 of Jeju's 43 neighborhoods.

Is Ara-dong lively in the evening?

28.2% of tourist card spending happens between 6 pm and midnight, #30 of 43.

Is Ara-dong a tourist area or a local area?

Tourists account for 18.6% of card sales there, so it is mostly a local residential area.

When is Ara-dong least crowded?

On a 5-year average (2019, 2022–2025), the quietest month is Apr and the busiest is Aug.

Has Ara-dong grown since before COVID?

Tourist visits in 2025 were 58% higher than in 2019 (#11 of 43).

Who should stay in Ara-dong?

It suits people who like moderately busy areas and people who want a local residential feel.
