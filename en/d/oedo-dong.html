FAQ

Is Oedo-dong busy with tourists?

Domestic tourists visited 1.6M times in one year (Aug 2025–Jul 2026), #30 of Jeju's 43 neighborhoods.

Is Oedo-dong lively in the evening?

38.4% of tourist card spending happens between 6 pm and midnight, #15 of 43.

Is Oedo-dong a tourist area or a local area?

Tourists account for 23.6% of card sales there, so it is mostly a local residential area.

When is Oedo-dong least crowded?

On a 5-year average (2019, 2022–2025), the quietest month is Mar and the busiest is Aug.

Has Oedo-dong grown since before COVID?

Tourist visits in 2025 were 71% higher than in 2019 (#9 of 43).

Who should stay in Oedo-dong?
