FAQ

Is Daecheon-dong busy with tourists?

Domestic tourists visited 3.4M times in one year (Aug 2025–Jul 2026), #17 of Jeju's 43 neighborhoods.

Is Daecheon-dong lively in the evening?

40.8% of tourist card spending happens between 6 pm and midnight, #9 of 43.

Is Daecheon-dong a tourist area or a local area?

Tourists account for 46.9% of card sales there, so it is a mix of tourists and locals.

When is Daecheon-dong least crowded?

On a 5-year average (2019, 2022–2025), the quietest month is Mar and the busiest is Aug.

Has Daecheon-dong grown since before COVID?

Tourist visits in 2025 were 54% higher than in 2019 (#12 of 43).

Who should stay in Daecheon-dong?
