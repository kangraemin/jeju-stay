Where to stay in Jeju: 43 neighborhoods compared

Which part of Jeju should you stay in? Compare Jeju's 43 towns and neighborhoods with mobile, card and navigation data from the Jeju Tourism Organization.

The interactive map, quiz and comparison are in Korean. This page summarizes the same data in English.

Open the interactive map (Korean) →

Key findings

  • 35.5% of all tourist visits go to just five places: Yongdam 2-dong, Aewol-eup, Andeok-myeon, Jocheon-eup, Yeon-dong.
  • Evening life differs a lot: Jeongbang-dong sees 60.2% of tourist spending after 6 pm, Udo-myeon only 5.2%.
  • Most tourist-heavy: Udo-myeon (84.4% of card sales). Most local: Ildo 2-dong (9.7%).
  • In autumn (Oct–Nov 2025), Saebyeol Oreum alone drew 30.0% of cars arriving at 97 oreum (volcanic cones).
  • 2019 → 2025: tourist visits to Jungang-dong rose +181% (Ildo 1-dong +151%, Dodu-dong +145%), while Udo-myeon fell 25% and Seongsan-eup 21%.
  • Foreign card spending: in Iho-dong, 90% comes from Taiwan. In Nohyeong-dong and Yeon-dong, China leads with 47% and 43%.
